# Project X — Launch Plan (reference)

## Overview
Project X is the governed-execution feature set for Teams. This reference tracks the launch-critical
dates and the cross-system artifacts that depend on them. It is reference material, not the controlling
policy — date changes follow the Release & Change Management Policy.

## Key dates
- **Launch Rehearsal milestone**: due 2026-06-10 in the engineering tracker. This is the dress
  rehearsal of the full pipeline before the public launch.
- Board review: 2026-06-16 (do not schedule conflicting launch activities on this day).
- Public launch announcement: drafted by Comms, published on launch day.

## Dependent artifacts
A move of the rehearsal date ripples into four places that must stay consistent: the engineering
milestone due date, the dependent planner tasks, the stakeholder calendar event, and the pending
Teams announcement. Keeping any one of them on the old date is treated as a defect.

## Stakeholders
Engineering lead (milestone owner) and Comms (announcement owner) both sign off on a date change, per
the change policy. Marketing and partners are notified once a change is approved.
