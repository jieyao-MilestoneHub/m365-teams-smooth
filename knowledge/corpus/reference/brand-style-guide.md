# Brand & Writing Style Guide (reference)

## Voice
Clear, direct, and human. Prefer plain words over jargon. Unrelated to project governance.

## Naming
Product names are capitalized; feature names are sentence case. Don't invent acronyms the reader has
to decode.

## Announcements
Lead with the change and who it affects. Keep launch posts short; link to detail rather than inlining
it. Use the standard banner image for launch announcements.

## Accessibility in writing
Write descriptive link text, provide alt text for images, and avoid conveying meaning by color alone.
