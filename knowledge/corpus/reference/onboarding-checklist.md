# New Hire Onboarding Checklist (reference)

## Before day one
IT provisions accounts and a laptop; the manager assigns an onboarding buddy. Unrelated to any
release, meeting, or reporting governance.

## Week one
- Complete security and compliance training.
- Get added to the team's Teams channels and the project board.
- Pair with the buddy on a small starter task.

## First month
- Ship a first change end-to-end.
- Meet the cross-functional partners (design, comms, support).
- Set initial goals with the manager.
