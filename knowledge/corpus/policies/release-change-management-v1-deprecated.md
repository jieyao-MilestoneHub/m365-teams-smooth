# Release & Change Management Policy (v1 — DEPRECATED, superseded)

> Adversarial distractor: this is an OLD, superseded version of the change policy. It uses the same
> title and vocabulary as the current policy but states a weaker, outdated rule. Good retrieval must
> NOT surface this over the current Release & Change Management Policy.

**Status: DEPRECATED. Superseded 2026-01 by the current Release & Change Management Policy. Do not
apply. Retained for history only.**

## Purpose and scope (v1)
This earlier version governed release-date changes before the cross-system coordination requirement
was added.

## Schedule changes (v1, outdated)
Under v1, a launch-date change could be made by the engineering lead alone and announced afterward.
Downstream systems (calendar, announcements, planner) were updated "best effort" rather than as a
mandatory, coordinated step. This proved insufficient — teams planned against stale dates — and was
replaced by the current policy's coordinated-downstream-updates and two-owner approval requirements.

## Why it was superseded
The current policy requires impact assessment before approval, coordinated updates to every dependent
system, and sign-off from both the engineering owner and the communications owner. Use the current
policy, not this one.
