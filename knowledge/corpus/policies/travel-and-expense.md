# Travel & Expense Policy

## Purpose
This policy covers business travel booking, per-diem rates, and expense reimbursement. It is unrelated
to release management, meetings governance, or status reporting.

## Booking travel
Book flights and hotels through the corporate travel portal. Economy for flights under six hours;
premium economy is permitted on longer haul with manager approval.

## Per diem and meals
Per-diem rates follow the published city tiers. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Itemized receipts are
required for any single expense over the receipt threshold.

## Submitting expenses
Submit expenses within 30 days of the trip in the expense tool. Reimbursements are paid on the next
payroll cycle after approval.
