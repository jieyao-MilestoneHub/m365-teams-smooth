# Information Security Incident Response Policy

## Purpose
This policy governs how suspected security incidents are reported, triaged, and resolved. It is a
security-operations policy, distinct from vendor access governance and from release management.

## Reporting an incident
Anyone who suspects a breach, data exposure, or account compromise reports it immediately to the
security on-call channel. Do not investigate alone; do not delete evidence.

## Triage and severity
Security triages each report and assigns a severity. High-severity incidents page the on-call
responder and open a coordinated response bridge.

## Containment and recovery
Contain first (isolate affected accounts/systems), then eradicate and recover. A post-incident review
captures the timeline, root cause, and corrective actions.

## Communication
External or customer-impacting incidents follow the disclosure process led by Legal and Comms; do not
communicate externally without sign-off.
