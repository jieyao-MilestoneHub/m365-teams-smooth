# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## Purpose
This policy sets expectations for remote and hybrid working arrangements. It is unrelated to release
management, meeting follow-through, or status reporting.

## Core hours and availability
Teams agree on overlapping core hours for collaboration. Outside core hours, asynchronous communication
is the default; no one is expected to be online continuously.

## Equipment and home office
The company provides a standard laptop and a home-office stipend. Equipment is tracked in the asset
register and returned on offboarding.

## In-office days
Hybrid teams choose anchor days for in-person collaboration. Anchor days are a team agreement, not a
company mandate.
