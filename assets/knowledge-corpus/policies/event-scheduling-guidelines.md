# Meeting & Event Scheduling Guidelines

> Near-miss by design: this is about *booking* meetings and rooms, not about changing a committed
> launch/milestone date (that is the Release & Change Management Policy). It exists so retrieval has a
> same-vocabulary distractor — "schedule", "reschedule", "event", "calendar" — that is not the
> controlling policy for a launch date change.

## Purpose
These guidelines help teams book meetings, reserve rooms, and avoid calendar collisions for routine
gatherings. They cover everyday scheduling hygiene, not governance of committed release dates.

## Booking rooms and online meetings
Reserve a room through the facilities calendar; for hybrid meetings, always attach a Teams link.
Recurring meetings should pick a stable weekly slot. Avoid back-to-back bookings without a buffer.

## Rescheduling a routine meeting
To move a routine meeting, propose a new slot in the calendar, check attendee availability, and send
the update. Routine meeting moves need no approval — they are not controlled changes. (A launch or
milestone date change is different and is governed separately.)

## Time zones and working hours
Schedule across time zones within shared working hours where possible; record the canonical time in
UTC in the invite body.
