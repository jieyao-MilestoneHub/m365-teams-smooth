# Release & Change Management Policy

## Purpose and scope
This policy governs changes to committed launch dates, release milestones, and the cross-system
schedule that depends on them (engineering milestones, customer-facing announcements, marketing
calendars, and partner timelines). It applies whenever a launch is proposed to slip, advance, or
otherwise change.

## Schedule changes are controlled changes
A change to a committed launch or milestone date is a controlled change, not an informal adjustment.
It requires impact assessment, the right approvals, and coordinated downstream updates before it is
considered effective. Silent or unilateral date changes are not permitted.

## Impact assessment before approval
Before a launch-date change is approved, assess second-order impact across systems: dependent
engineering milestones and blocking issues, customer commitments tied to the original date, scheduled
announcements and calendar events, and any contractual or renewal implications. A date change is only
sound when its full downstream impact is understood and recorded.

## Coordinated downstream updates
An approved launch slip must propagate consistently to every dependent system: update the engineering
milestone due date, shift dependent task dates, reschedule or update the customer/stakeholder calendar
events, and update the announcement. Leaving any dependent system on the old date is a defect — the
schedule must stay internally consistent across engineering, calendar, planner, and announcements.

## Approval and quorum
A launch-date change requires sign-off from the responsible product/engineering owner; changes that
affect customer-facing commitments or announcements additionally require the communications or
customer-facing owner. The approvers scale with the blast radius of the change.

## Notification and stakeholders
Stakeholders affected by the change are notified through the normal channel (for example a Teams
announcement update), so that no team continues to plan against a superseded date.

## Audit and rollback
The change decision — request, impact evidence, approvers, verdict, and the before/after schedule
snapshot — is recorded in an append-only audit trail. Rollback guidance (how to restore the prior
dates) is captured as advisory data and is never executed automatically.
